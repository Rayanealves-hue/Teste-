Why Rent-To-Own?

  • Our Rent-To-Own enables you to afford that building you need, immediately!
  • Our Rent-To-Own eliminates hassles, limited access and travel time/fuel cost to store your belongings elsewhere, like a mini storage unit.
  • With our Rent-To-Own, you'll own your own custom shed, unlike mini storage units, where you'll never own it!
  • Our Rent-To-Own costs less per day than most people spend on snacks.
  • You're only responsible for a small rent payment prior to delivery.
  • Our flexible non-binding agreement allows you a no-strings return or early purchase option.

What are the main benefits of rent-to-own?

  • Immediate enjoyment & use of the structure with only a small payment due each month.
  • Flexibility: if you move or no longer need the structure, there's no obligation anymore. Free, no-strings-attached return.
  • Rent for a few years and keep it for a lifetime (you never get to keep a mini storage!), or upgrade to a nicer, newer structure every year for small monthly rent payments.
