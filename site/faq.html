How far do you deliver?

Free delivery anywhere within 250 miles of Liverpool, PA (excluding NY). Beyond 250 miles, delivery is $5 per mile, one way. Our service area is the entire United States, excluding New York.

Can you remove my old shed?

Yes. If you are replacing an old shed with a Classic shed, we will take care of removing your old shed before your new one is delivered and installed. There may be a charge of $100 to $300, depending on how dilapidated the old shed is.

How long does installation take?

Your shed is delivered and installed the same day by our careful experts, placed exactly to your specifications and ready for use.

How should I prepare my site?

Choose one of three options when you order: a professionally installed stone pad (not available on rent-to-own), our shed leveling service with concrete blocks and treated shims (available on rent-to-own), or free lawn delivery. See stone pad prices.

How does rent-to-own work?

You get free delivery with only one month's rent down. We offer 60, 48, 36 or 24 month rent-to-own on LP SmartSide and custom vinyl structures, starting at $100.93 a month, plus sales tax. Over $15,000, 10% down is required, and the maximum rent-to-own contract is $25,000. You can purchase the building at any time, or cancel the contract by simply returning it. No strings attached.

What are the main benefits of rent-to-own?
  • Immediate enjoyment & use of the structure with only a small payment due each month.
  • Flexibility: if you move or no longer need the structure, there's no obligation anymore. Free, no-strings-attached return.
  • Rent for a few years and keep it for a lifetime (you never get to keep a mini storage!), or upgrade to a nicer, newer structure every year for small monthly rent payments.
Do you offer financing?

Yes. Zero down financing through Backyard Finance. Larger LP SmartSide buildings built on site have 120-month financing. Call 814.470.4494 for details.

How can I pay?

By finance, rent-to-own, cash, check or credit card.

What does the Lifetime Warranty cover?

Your vinyl or LP SmartSide building is covered in full for 40 years. The warranty is non-transferable and does not cover acts of nature, normal wear and tear, misuse, accidents, improper site preparation, or anything that is not part of the building's structure. Timber frame pavilions are sold without warranty.

Can I customize my building?

Yes. Add porches, doors, windows, colors and more. For a modest upcharge, we can use any vinyl brand or color on the market.

Where can I see your buildings?

At our sales centers in Port Trevorton (Selinsgrove Sales Center), Beaver Springs, Clarion and Muncy, Pennsylvania, open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. See the addresses.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. When your friends or family buy, you collect $50 and they get $50 off.
