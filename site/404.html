About Us

Who we are: Classic Shed Builders LLC is a local family business handcrafting sheds, garages and timber frame pavilions at our manufacturing plant in Liverpool, Pennsylvania.

For most companies, warranties are carefully worded documents designed to legally protect them, not their customers. Instead of following standard industry practices, we decided to take responsibility for the long term, with a true lifetime warranty on all craftsmanship and materials.

If you aren't quite sure what you want or need, we'll help you figure it out without pressuring you into a sale. And when we deliver your shed, we'll make sure the building is set exactly where you want it before we leave.

Delivery & Site Prep

Delivered and set exactly where you want it

Free delivery anywhere in Pennsylvania and 50 miles beyond state lines (excluding NY). Our drivers carry a Mule, a maneuverable, forklift-like device that gets your building into tight spots a truck may not be able to go.

Option 1 · Recommended

Professionally installed stone pad

4" limestone, #1 crusher run, tamped and laser leveled, or a timbered site with treated timbers and landscape fabric. Not available on rent-to-own.

Option 2 · Available on rent-to-own

Shed leveling service

At time of delivery or prior, we level with 8" x 16" concrete blocks and treated shims, using special leveling equipment.

Option 3 · Free

Lawn delivery

We place your structure at the exact spot you choose, directly on the lawn. Adjustments to the corners will be needed over time, done by you with levers or jacks.

Lifetime Warranty

Built for you. Backed for life.

At Classic Shed Builders LLC, we stand behind every building with a Lifetime Warranty that covers your building in full, for 40 years. The expected longevity of our buildings is 40 years with vinyl, metal and LP SmartSide. This warranty is non-transferable.

Not covered: acts of nature (storms, winds, floods, hail, fire, earthquakes, lightning); normal wear and tear, misuse, accidents or improper site preparation; landscaping, vegetation or anything that is not part of the building's structure. Timber frame pavilions are sold without warranty.
