About Us

Our family-owned business started like most plain-community businesses do: apprenticing at an uncle's shop and learning from experienced builders. Over 23 years ago, we started in slowly, building for locals, but quickly saw the need in the Northeast for well-built structures.

Meeting people and ensuring that every single one of our customers is 100% satisfied has been very rewarding, and continues to be our vision.

Delivery & Installation

We take care of everything

You don't have to worry about removing your old shed, preparing your site, or installing your brand new shed. We'll take care of it.

Shed Removal

If you are replacing an old shed with a Classic shed, we will take care of removing your old shed before your new one is delivered and installed. Leaving the heavy lifting to us.

Site Preparation

A long-lasting and well kept shed starts with a good foundation. We offer preferred gravel site preparation, as well as other options, to create the perfect landing place for your new shed.

Installation

Your shed is delivered and installed the same day by our careful experts. By the end of the day, your shed will be placed exactly to your specifications and ready for use!
