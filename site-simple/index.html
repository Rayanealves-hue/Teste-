Delivery & Warranty

Delivered, set and backed for life

Free delivery anywhere in Pennsylvania and 50 miles beyond state lines (excluding NY).

Free delivery with the Mule

Our drivers carry a unique piece of equipment called a Mule, a maneuverable, forklift-like device that gets your new building into tight spots a truck may not be able to go. The Mule also has less impact on your yard than a truck or trailer.

Is access a problem? We'll build anywhere with our crews.

Before delivery

  • Remove barriers such as fences, low limbs, stumps, mud and utility wires under 13' off the ground.
  • Check with your local building code office about permits and setbacks.
  • Mark the exact spot for your building with flags.
Plan your building

6 helpful steps to design your structure

  1. Discuss access and site. Is access a problem? Need to build on site?
  2. Choose size. Tip: it's always better to go with a size larger than you think you may need now.
  3. Select options, from practical to decorative.
  4. Specify windows and doors.
  5. Pick a finish and color scheme.
  6. Choose a payment option: finance, cash, check, rent-to-own or credit card.
Site preparation

3 leveling options

A properly prepared site will ensure the longevity of your structure. Make your choice at time of ordering.

Option 1 · Recommended

Professionally installed stone pad

Stone base: 4" limestone, slightly above ground level, #1 crusher run, tamped and laser leveled.

Timbered site: 4" limestone with treated timbers containing the edges and landscape fabric underneath. Laser leveled.

Not available on rent-to-own.

Option 2 · Available on rent-to-own

Shed leveling service

At time of delivery or prior, we level with 8" x 16" concrete blocks and treated shims, using special leveling equipment. You're buying our responsibility to level the shed up to 16".

Not recommended on structures with garage doors.

Option 3 · Free

Lawn delivery

We place your structure at the exact spot you choose, directly on the lawn. The 4x4 timbers that are part of the shed are pressure treated. Adjustments to the corners to align the doors will be needed over time, done by you with levers or jacks.
